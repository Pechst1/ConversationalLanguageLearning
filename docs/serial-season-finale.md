# Tentpole Reference — Season Finale

## Beat Sheet
- State in: The season's private promises have all changed shape, and the full group can finally see the consequences.
- Opening pressure: A concrete disruption at Le Mistral forces every open thread into the same room.
- Comic engine: Each character tries to protect someone else and accidentally reveals one truth too many.
- Emotional turn: The learner names what this chosen life in French now asks of them and what the group owes one another.
- Payoff: Give every completed arc one visible consequence without reopening its resolved question.
- Hook out: End on one specific new problem that belongs to the next season, while keeping the same cast and relationships intact.

## State Out
- Season-one promises are paid off rather than reset.
- Existing relationships and learner choices carry forward.
- The doorway into season two is concrete, unresolved, and actionable.
