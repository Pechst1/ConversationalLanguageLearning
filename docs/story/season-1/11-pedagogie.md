# Pedagogy hooks per tentpole day

*For WP-104 («La rubrique du jour») to attach its theme work to the story later.*

**The rule.** The scene comes first. These hooks describe what each scene *already asks the learner to do*. If an objective would change a line, a beat or a choice, the objective moves and the scene stays. Grammar is corrected in the margin and never gates the plot.

| Day | Natural situation | Can-do (the learner can…) | Words the scene needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 A** | Walking into a café where you are mistaken for someone | Say who you are and why you have come; correct a misunderstanding | la grand-mère, la clé, l'appartement, vendre, venir pour, je suis… de la famille, le premier étage |
| **T1 B** | A stranger asks about your plans; strangers ask to read your letter | Say what you plan to do and how sure you are; decide whom to trust | une offre, valable jusqu'à, le notaire, pas encore, peut-être, rentrer, une semaine, montrer |
| **T2 A** | Exploring a room that belonged to someone | Describe what you see; compare what people say with what you find | le calendrier, un rendez-vous, prévu, la boîte, la photo, derrière, à mon avis, fatigué(e), oublier |
| **T2 B** | Someone is measuring a family home for sale | Say what you want to keep and why; talk about how long you'll stay | garder, vider, le manteau, les photos, rester, combien de temps, un double (de clé), prêter |
| **T3 A** | An office counter, then a friend's request | Make or refuse a promise; ask a question that challenges a story | promettre, jamais, signer, le propriétaire, la pétition, le zinc, je ne peux pas, j'essaie |
| **T3 B** | A friend's plan, and persuading another friend | Respond to a proposal; persuade someone with a reason that matters to them | la coopérative, les parts, mille euros, la banque, inviter, pour elle, garder ses ennemis près de soi, parce que |
| **T4 A** | A party, and then a photograph | Read evidence in a picture; say where and when something happened | en haut / en bas, la cuisine, le feu, à onze heures, la nuit, le néon, à l'envers |
| **T4 B** | Being accused in public; hearing a confession | Answer an accusation truthfully; express sorrow and thanks; say what you feel about what someone did | savoir (je ne savais pas), malade, pardon, merci, mentir, protéger, en vouloir à quelqu'un, les dégâts |
| **T5 A** | A friend tells you she is leaving | Say something true about how you feel; react to someone's news | partir, rester, content(e) pour toi, je ne veux pas que…, vouloir quelque chose pour quelqu'un, le rêve, Berlin, un an |
| **T5 B** | Finding a message you weren't meant to read yet | Work out whether a message is an invitation, an apology or a goodbye; ask why someone didn't tell you | une invitation, des excuses, un adieu, quand, me le dire, se disputer, un secret, le visage |
| **T6 A** | An old man's account; deciphering handwriting | Listen to someone's side of a story; say what you think about it; read a difficult note | le dimanche, demander, réfléchir, le lendemain, avoir honte, le toit, les escaliers, un musée, je ne suis pas d'accord |
| **T6 B** | Deciding what to do with evidence before a meeting | Explain a decision and its reasons to the people it affects | montrer, publier, garder, à qui, punir, la vérité, dix heures, la promesse de vente |
| **T7 A** | New Year's Eve: persuading a friend; midnight | Persuade someone who resists; make a wish or a confession | accepter, seul(e), la banquette, ma mère, la table de pause, bonne année, promettre, une semaine |
| **T7 B** | A friend asks for nothing and lets you choose | Say what you want for someone else; make a decision and name its cost | fatiguée, la mer, libre, choisir, vendre à, la dette, l'atelier, c'est trop |
| **T8 A** | A goodbye to a place, and a letter | Say goodbye to a place and to people; read a personal letter | fermer, la clé, merci pour tout, la première maison, heureuse, oublier, laisser, quelqu'un |
| **T8 B** | A railway platform, and an inscription | Say goodbye to someone you love; read a short inscription and understand where it leads | le train, revenir, s'écrire, se promettre, derrière le tableau, toujours, la rue |

**Notes for WP-104**

- **What the dossier can do.** The words above are the *floor*: the scene cannot be played without them. A day's dossier (the practice around the episode) can pre-teach them in the atelier before the page and recycle them after it. It must never quiz them *inside* the page.
- **Bands.** A1 learners meet the same scenes through the recognition mechanics: pictures, taps, one short line. The A2 lines in the scripts are the reference text for the band ladder. B1 lines show where richer structures fit naturally.
