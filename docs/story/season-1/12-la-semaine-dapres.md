# Épilogue · «La semaine d'après»

*WP-132B proposal (2026-10-05), content program D8: the authored epilogue week after T8. Owner approval required: nothing here is canon until approved. Same conventions as the tentpoles: **A2** is the line, **B1** only where it differs; A1, B2 and C1 variants live in `levels_epilogue.json`.*

*Content program D8: the authored epilogue week after T8. Three two-day pages, played in order after T8 B (Sat 9 – Fri 15 January). Each finale branch keeps its own consequences: the painting is with you (garder), hidden by Gus (partager) or sold to an old gentleman (laisser_partir); Lila writes from Berlin; Camille still says vous; Marchand's Sundays continue when they were offered. Every branch converges on the same open door, the rue de Lancry, and the season's open question («Who is L.?») is left for the next authored season.*
