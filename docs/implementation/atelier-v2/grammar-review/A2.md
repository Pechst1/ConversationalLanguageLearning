# Grammar review: A2 band (package G-A2, 2026-10-03)

Shards: `app/data/grammar_review/units_A2.json` (36 units: 34 reviewed, 2 new), `app/data/rule_cards/fr2_A2.json` (36 cards, `rule-cards-v2`).
All units are `review_status: reviewed`. Every unit has a `regex:` detector, and each detector matches its own x-ray sentence and every anchor.

## Changes that apply across the band

- **Traps.** Every `main_traps` item is now a pure `✗ wrong → ✓ right` pair, so `contrast_pairs` turns all three into exercise pairs. Before, about a third were prose. Each unit now has one trap aimed at English speakers and one at German speakers where such a trap exists. Examples: *je suis nagé* (ich bin geschwommen), *je lui aide* (helfen + dative), *plus cher comme* (wie/als), *Habite Marie ici ?*, *je me sens meilleur*, *il parle lent*.
- **X-ray marks.** No span uses `…` any more (`_mark` skips those). Spans no longer overlap, because the second mark of an overlapping pair was silently dropped. Roles were renamed so `_role_color` gives meaningful colours: `agreement` and `event` are red, `background` is yellow, and `negative_*` is blue.
- **Ordering.** `PC_NEGATION`, `PASSE_RECENT` and `EN_TRAIN_DE` are now `is_foundation=true`. Under the current `is_foundation DESC, difficulty_order` sort, every A2.1 unit now comes before every A2.2 unit. The build script checks this. E-2 should still sort by sub-band.
- **Rule cards.** Each card has a speaker from the drawn cast, an example, a 3-language rule, a pattern, a contrast, a 3-language `more`, numbered `how` steps, 3–4 examples, 3 traps and `contrast_with`.
  - Tense units use a `table` with a model verb. Silent endings are grey: `parl[ai]{s}`, `parler[on]{t}`, `[tou]{s} les jours`.
  - Agreement endings are marked as rule-carrying (`allé[e]`), and `more` says that they are silent.
  - German-specific remarks go only into the `de` strings, which are aimed at German speakers, and English-specific ones only into `en`.
- **Vocabulary.** I checked anchors, x-rays and card examples against the lexicon bands. I replaced *appart*, *renseignement*, *déplacer*, *en pièce jointe*, *à l'avance*, *repeindre*, *alarme* and *tranquillement* with A1–A2 words.

## Detector notes

- **FR2_A22_IMP_VS_PC (D9 approximation).** The detector needs an imparfait form with a pronoun subject (or *c'*) and a passé composé form in the same sentence, in either order. The passé composé can be avoir + participle, être + movement verb, or pronominal. Limits:
  - It misses a story told across several sentences.
  - It misses an imparfait with a noun subject when the passé composé is not in the same sentence.
  - It also fires on reported speech (*il a dit qu'il était…*), where both tenses really do co-occur.
  - The pattern is long (about 3.3k characters) because it is composed from the imparfait and passé composé parts.
- **FR2_A22_FUTUR_SIMPLE** fires on any futur form, regular or irregular. **FR2_A22_FUTUR_IRREGULAR** fires only on the irregular stems. So *nous irons* counts as evidence for both units.
- **Probes.** The scratch probe set has 270 sentences (157 positive, 113 negative), including the audit's *Nous irons*, *tu feras*, *ils viendront*, plus traps such as *il y a ici* and *vous avez du pain*. No unit has a false negative or a false positive in that set. Firing over the 612 catalogue sentences:
  - The broadest detectors are PC_AVOIR (106, all real passé composé uses) and IMPARFAIT (45).
  - The narrowest is SI_ON (1).

## Doubts and handoffs

- **Can-dos.** The new units are in no can-do yet. `fr_core_can_dos_v2.json` needs an A2.1 "know a fact / know a person" can-do and an A2.2 "suggest a plan" can-do. That belongs to integration or T-1.
- **Item banks.** The new units have no item bank and no séance challenge.
- **Card labels.** Pattern labels such as *décor*, *féminin + -ment* and *+ consonne* are French, as in the v1 cards. If F-1 wants all labels in the interface language, they have to be localised there, because the schema has `label` as a plain string.
- **`[(e)]`.** The `PC_PRONOMINAL` table uses `levé[(e)]` for the optional feminine. That renders the parentheses red; F-1 may prefer `levé{(e)}`.
- **Ordering.** The fix uses `is_foundation`. If E-2 sorts by sub-band, the three A2.1 units could go back to `false`. I left them `true` because they really are core.
- **SI_ON requires a `?`.** On transcribed speech without punctuation it will miss. That is deliberate, so that it does not credit *si on avait…, on partirait* (B1.1).
- **v1 cards.** The 24 v1 cards in `grammar_rule_cards.json` are untouched. The good material from the A2 ones (Lila's imparfait/passé composé card, Marin's relatives, the PC tables) was reused and split along `french_core_grammar_v1_to_v2.tsv`.
