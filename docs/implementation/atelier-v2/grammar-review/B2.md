# Grammar review: B2 band (package G-B2, 2026-10-03)

Shards: `app/data/grammar_review/units_B2.json` (46 units: 45 reviewed, 1 new) and `app/data/rule_cards/fr2_B2.json` (46 cards, `rule-cards-v2`).
Every unit is `review_status: reviewed` and has a `regex:` detector. Each detector matches its own x-ray sentence, every anchor, and every example on its card.

## Changes across the band

- **Traps.** Every `main_traps` item is now a clean `✗ wrong → ✓ right` pair. Before, about two thirds of the B2 traps were prose. Where a real interference error exists, each unit has one trap for English speakers and one for German speakers. Examples:
  - *le colis devient livré* (wird), *je laisse réparer ma voiture* (lassen), *je me suis laissé couper les cheveux*
  - *je lui l'ai donné* (es ihm), *vient Marie ?*, *le ministre doit avoir démissionné* (soll)
  - *je manque toi*, *j'ai eu mes cheveux coupés*, *puisque 2019*, *tellement beaucoup de*, *le suspect est dit être*
- **X-ray marks.**
  - Four marks used `...` and were never drawn: INDIC_SUBJ, CAUSATIVE, REPORTED_SEQUENCE and FORMAL_INVERSION. All four are now contiguous spans.
  - The overlapping marks in CAUSATIVE and FORMAL_INVERSION are gone.
  - The build runs the engine's own `_mark`, which checks that every mark is drawn.
  - Roles were renamed so that `_role_color` gives meaningful colours: trigger and concession are blue; result, event, agreement and subjunctive are red; antecedent is yellow.
- **Ordering.**
  - All 22 B2.1 units, plus the new one, are now `is_foundation=true`. Before this change, 13 B2.1 units sorted after the B2.2 foundation units SI_MIXED, REPORTED_SEQUENCE and FORMAL_INVERSION.
  - Under `is_foundation DESC, teaching_order`, every B2.1 unit now comes before every B2.2 unit.
  - The build checks this ordering, and that teaching orders are unique.
- **Rule cards.** Each card has:
  - a speaker, an example, a three-language rule (`rule.en` is at most 20 words) and a pattern;
  - a contrast, a `more` in three languages, numbered `how` steps, 3–4 examples, 3 traps and `contrast_with`.

  Tense and mood units use a `table` (4–8 rows, with `note` en/de/fr): subjonctif passé, conditionnel passé, futur antérieur, the passive in all tenses, infinitif passé, reported-speech shifts, and the irregular passé simple. All `rule_short_*` are at most 20 words, and de/fr differ from en.
- **Anchors.**
  - Anchors that the unit's own point could not show were replaced. APRES_QUE's *avant qu'il ne pleuve* belongs to B1.2; EN_DANS's *dans cinq minutes* belongs to A2.1; SUBJ_EMOTION's *se passe* is identical in the indicative.
  - Most units gained a 4th anchor.

## Detector notes (D9 approximations)

- **INDIC_SUBJ.** The detector credits only the mood that matches the trigger:
  - a hope/probability trigger + an indicative form (futur, imparfait, est/a/va…);
  - a wish/possibility trigger + a distinctive subjonctif form.

  It misses regular -er verbs whose two moods are identical.
- **SI_MIXED.** The detector matches:
  - si + plus-que-parfait with a conditionnel présent;
  - si + imparfait with a conditionnel passé.

  Either clause order counts. Pure type 2 and type 3 sentences do not match.
- **NOMINALISATION.** The detector matches about 150 derived nouns + *de*, or *-ation/-ition/-ssion* + *de*, with a stop-list (station, question, situation…). It cannot know whether the nominalisation was deliberate.
- **VERB_MEANING_PREP.** The detector matches the five construction pairs. It credits the form, not the intended meaning.
- **CONDITIONAL_HEARSAY.** The detector matches a source + a conditionnel, or a sentence-initial noun subject + *aurait/serait…* with no *si*. A third-person unreal past without *si* can match.
- **Pattern length.** SI_TYPE3 (about 4.7k characters) and SI_MIXED (about 6.8k) are long because they are composed from shared parts. SUBJ_PAST, COND_PAST and FUT_ANT are about 2–3k.
- **Probes.** The scratch probe set has 549 sentences (311 positive, 238 negative): 0 misses, 0 false hits. Over the 663 catalogue sentences (A1–B1 shards plus the TSV), the remaining B2 hits on lower-band sentences are genuine shared forms:
  - B1.2 passive passé composé;
  - B1.2 passé simple *fut/fit*;
  - B1.1 *ces photos, je les ai prises*;
  - A2.1 *ferme-t-il*.
