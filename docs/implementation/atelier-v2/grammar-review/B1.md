# Grammar review — B1 (G-B1, 2026-10-03)

Scope: the 40 B1 units of `templates/french_core_grammar_v2.tsv` (FR2_B11_*, FR2_B12_*) plus one new unit. Outputs: `app/data/grammar_review/units_B1.json` (41 units, every TSV column, `review_status=reviewed`) and `app/data/rule_cards/fr2_B1.json` (41 rule cards v2+, en/de/fr).

Overall: the draft texts were already accurate; most changes are completions (missing forms, people vs things, register), traps rewritten where they were not real errors, contrast partners filled in, and every detector re-checked. Every unit now has a `regex:` detector that matches all its anchors and its x-ray sentence (and every example on its rule card). Detectors were also run against all 612 catalogue sentences of the other units and against hand-written negatives (scratch harness); new hits are genuine uses of the form.

## Doubts and approximations

- **FR2_B11_NARRATION** (D9): the regex credits a plus-que-parfait plus an imparfait or passé composé in one sentence. A two-tense story (imparfait + passé composé only) is credited to A2.2 IMP_VS_PC instead. Reported speech with a plus-que-parfait («il a dit qu'il avait fini») also matches; it is a narrative past, so this is accepted.
- **FR2_B12_CONSEQUENCE**: still fires on «Ce que je ne comprends pas, c'est pourquoi il est parti» (interrogative pourquoi). Python `re` has no variable-width lookbehind, so it can't exclude this cleanly without making the matched span very long.
- **FR2_B12_PASSIVE**: an agentless noun subject + être + -é participle counts («le magasin est fermé», the passif d'état). Common adjectival participles (fatigué, occupé, marié, pressé, désolé, débordé…) are excluded, but others («le café est sucré») may still match.
- **FR2_B12_SUBJ_FORM / SUBJ_IRREGULAR**: nous/vous subjunctives (que nous parlions, que nous allions) look the same as the imparfait, so they count only after a trigger (il faut, vouloir, pour/avant/sans, ne pas penser, emotion words…). A bare «que nous parlions» with no trigger is not credited, by design. Imperatives sois/soyez/ayez share the subjunctive form and are read too.
- **FR2_B11_SI_TYPE1**: a present-tense main clause («Si tu viens, je suis content») is not detected. Only futur, aller + infinitive and imperative main clauses are, because a present can't be told apart from other verbs by its shape.
- **Shape-based detectors** (conditional, future, participles) use stop-lists for look-alike nouns (vrai, bras, dîner, hiver, plaisir…). Rare look-alikes outside those lists can still match.
- **New unit FR2_B11_DE_PLUS_EN_PLUS**: integration must add the row to the TSV (teaching_order 885, sub_band B1.1). The B1 band count goes from 40 to 41; `tests/test_wp_l2_syllabus.py` treats band counts as approximate targets.
- **Reported orders** (il m'a dit de venir) were added to FR2_B12_REPORTED_PAST as a 4th anchor, rather than as a new unit.
- Nothing else was found missing at B1: the whole A1–B2 catalogue was checked against the DELF B1 inventory. Topics such as subjonctif after emotion, futur antérieur and causative faire sit at B2.1 by design.
