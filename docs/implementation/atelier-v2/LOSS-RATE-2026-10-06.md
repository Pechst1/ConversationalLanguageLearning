# Loss rate of generated days: diagnosis and fixes (2026-10-06)

Branch `exp/loss-rate` (base `a7fc563`). This package covers analysis, code and free dry runs only. It made no paid call and never set `SEASON_REPORT_LIVE`.

## 1. What the reads show

The three reads are WP-133a, WP-133b and the WP-133b re-read: 14 runs and 77 generated-day attempts, of which **31 were lost (40 %)**. Every loss recovered honestly.

The logged reason is the **last** attempt's reason. The earlier refused drafts of the same day were never recorded. The wrapper's log filter dropped the line that carried them (`every SceneDraft attempt refused …`).

### Where the losses fall

I re-read every transcript's day list and placed each attempt in its gap.

| Position of the attempt | Attempts | Lost | Rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **First day of a gap** (the day after a tentpole's Day B, retries of that day, and the continuation after the epilogue) | 36 | 23 | **64 %** |
| A later day inside a gap | 41 | 8 | 20 % |

- **All 8 `wrong_beat` losses and all 4 `chapter_not_advanced` losses fall on a first day.** That alone is 12 of the 31 losses.
- **The seeded later runs (5–8) lost 67 %.** Almost all their attempts were first days: after a loss the recovery jumps to the next tentpole, so the next attempt is a first day again.
  - Seeded first days lost 15 of 18 (83 %). Non-seeded first days lost 8 of 18 (44 %).
  - Most of the gap between those two rates is the position mix. The rest cannot be separated with n = 18 (see §4).

## 2. Diagnosis per reason

**Attempts per day.** `_approved` gets `ATELIER_STORY_MAX_ATTEMPTS` = 2 attempts, plus a bonus attempt only when a full 35 s window is left in the 75 s budget.
- In the live read, attempt 0 writes **two drafts in parallel** (`DUAL_DRAFTS_ENABLED`, setup and turn beats).
- The season critic runs after the guards. A critic refusal spends an attempt, but it never loses the day: the refused draft is kept and served.
- In practice a lost day means 3 or 4 drafts were refused.

Classes: (a) the brief under-specifies what the guard demands; (b) the brief and the guard contradict each other; (c) a schema or parse fragility; (d) a legitimate model miss.

## 3. What changed (code)

| Fix | Where |
|---|---|
| `brief.page_rules`: each guard's rule with today's values (beat; new or continuing chapter; retired questions; already-asked objectives and the overlap rule; speakers; two-hander voice; registers; narration address; objective size; field limits) | `living_story.season_page_rules`, merged into the brief in `story_context` |
| The director prompt: the brief outranks arcs, secrets and callbacks, **not** the chapter bookkeeping; follow `brief.page_rules` literally | `living_story.DIRECTOR` (SEASON SCRIPT paragraph) |
| A closed chapter's shape is no longer shown, or checked, for the next chapter's setup | `story_context`, `_validate_scene` |
| Sharper hints for `wrong_beat` (`_wrong_beat_hint`), `chapter_not_advanced`, `resolution_repeats_turn`, `unknown_panel_character` and `objective_too_thin` | `living_story._validate_scene` and `_check_objective_scope` |
| `brief.registers` follows `register.<id>` flags | `season/director.gap_brief` |
| Tolerant parsing of what carries no story: enum casing; «none» meaning no claim; unknown `capability_key`; unknown keys at the scene, chapter, panel and line levels; list overflow in `source_event_ids`, `lexicon` and `possible_developments`; lexicon field caps; `visual_direction` over 500 characters. **Learner-facing French is never cut. An unknown beat («aftermath») still fails.** | `living_story.tolerant_scene_json` |
| The checklist is lenient: threads cut to 4, notes cut at a word, a «none» premise means null | `season/director.SeasonChecklist` |
| A truncated page gets its own hint, and the next attempt gets `max_tokens` 8,000. The budget is raised only when truncation is proven in that call. | `living_story._json_call`, `_next_budget` |
| Every refused proposal (guard, schema or critic) goes to `REFUSAL_OBSERVERS`, with reason, hint, draft digest and output details | `living_story._refused`, `scene_digest` |
| The live-read wrapper records `refusals` (with the season position) and `refusal_reasons` in the run JSON, and keeps the `attempt refused` and `shape repaired` lines | `tests/test_wp133a_live_read.py` |
| `jump_to_day` produces a real learner's state (§4) | `season/admin.py` |

**No guard's substance changed.** The beat, chapter, two-hander (on its own chapter), register, departed-cast, agreement, objective-scope, resolution-overlap (still 0.45), unknown-speaker, spoiler and word-limit guards all refuse what they refused before. `lane_guards.py` and the departed and register guards are untouched.

## 4. Seeded runs (`jump_to_day`)

**Does the jump cause `wrong_beat` or `chapter_not_advanced`? No.**
- Every seeded run starts on a tentpole's Day A.
- So when the first gap day comes, its chapter state is exactly a real learner's: the tentpole opened the chapter, and Day B resolved it.
- The jump's only chapter difference is a *shorter* list of retired questions, which makes `chapter_not_advanced` less likely, not more.
- The seeded 67 % is mostly the position mix (§1).

**The jump did leave a state no learner has:**
- **Bare `state_out` only.** Turn sets that apply «whatever is said» and the solves' defaults were never applied. From T6 on, `s1.plan`, `s1.went_with_lila_to_marin`, `marchand.knows_why` and `camille.disagreements` were unset. That affects runs 7 and 8.
- **No required moments recorded** for the gaps already passed.
- **The cast's registers stayed at their opening values**, so Gus said «vous» after T3, until the first settle.

`jump_to_day` now:
1. settles every passed tentpole day with the runtime's own `_settle_tentpole` and no routed reply: turn sets, solve defaults and `state_out`. No choice is invented for the learner.
2. records the required moments of every gap passed **in full**. A jump into a gap leaves that gap's moments owed, which `test_a_director_that_skips_the_scheduled_moment…` relies on.
3. applies the registers.

`flags` still override everything. The docstring says all of this.

## 5. Expected effect (hypotheses, to be confirmed by a paid read)

| Reason | Losses in 77 | Expected after | Why |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `wrong_beat` + `chapter_not_advanced` | 12 | 2–5 | The contradiction is removed and the retry hint is coherent. The model may still keep the old chapter on some draws. |
| `invalid_story_output` | 7 | 3–5 | Only if the failing fields are the no-story ones. Learner-facing overflow is unaffected. Truncation is unproven. |
| `objective_too_thin` | 2 | 0–1 | The floor is now stated. |
| `resolution_repeats_turn` | 2 | 0–1 | The shared words are named, and «already asked» is in the brief. |
| `mixed_address_register` | 2 | 1–2 | A prompt-only change. The narration habit may persist. |
| `unknown_panel_character` | 2 | 0–1 | The brief no longer advertises non-cast ids. |
| `two_hander_crowded` | 1 | 0 | The shape mismatch is fixed. |
| others (`gendered_agreement`, `repeated_situation`, provider) | 3 | 3 | Legitimate misses or transient failures. |
| **All** | **31 (40 %)** | **≈ 9–18 (12–23 %)** | First-day loss from 64 % to about 15–35 %. |

**Only a paid read can confirm:**
- that gpt-5-mini follows `page_rules` on first gap days;
- which field `invalid_story_output` actually fails on, and whether `finish_reason` is `length`;
- that the longer brief (about 400 more input tokens) does not push other guards (`scene_too_long`, the critic) up;
- that an 8,000-token retry stays inside the 35 s window;
- whether seeded first days still lose more than non-seeded ones with the consistent state.

## 6. Proposed confirmation read (about US$0.60, three runs; not run)

Same harness and production configuration as WP-133b (`tests/test_wp133a_live_read.py`, two drafts, critic, lanes, catalogue v2, practice day on). Each run has a hard cap.

| # | Band | Days | Why | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C1.1 | 1–10 | Re-read 3 lost 3 of 6 (`objective_too_thin`, `invalid_story_output`, `wrong_beat`), all on first days. | 0.25 |
| 2 | B1.1 | 25–32 (seeded, new `jump_to_day`) | Run 5 lost 4 of 4 first days (`wrong_beat` ×2, `objective_too_thin`, `chapter_not_advanced`). | 0.20 |
| 3 | A1.1 | 1–10 | Re-read 1: `two_hander_crowded` and `invalid_story_output` inside g1. | 0.20 |

**Pass criteria:**
- at most 2 of about 8 first-day attempts lost;
- no `wrong_beat` or `chapter_not_advanced` whose refused draft continued a closed chapter (`refusals[].draft.chapter_closed` true with `beat` ≠ setup);
- every `invalid_story_output` refusal carries `output.finish_reason` and an excerpt.

Read `refusal_reasons` and `refusals` in each run's JSON. Example command for run 1:

```
WP133A_OUT=<dir> SEASON_REPORT=<dir>/C1.md SEASON_REPORT_LIVE=1 SEASON_REPORT_MAX_USD=0.25 \
SEASON_REPORT_DAYS=10 SEASON_REPORT_BAND=C1.1 python -m pytest tests/test_wp133a_live_read.py -q -s
```

For run 2, add `SEASON_REPORT_START=25` and set `SEASON_REPORT_DAYS=8`.
