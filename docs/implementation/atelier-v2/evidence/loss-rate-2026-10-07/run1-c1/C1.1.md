## Jour 1 — T1 · Le mauvais accueil · jour A · mercredi 11 novembre

**a.p1** — *Night rain on the quai de Valmy. The red neon MISTRAL on the corner. Toi from behind, with a suitcase and an envelope held under the coat against the rain. Through the café's window, Gus stands on the booth's bench mid-anecdote, and Marin and Lila look up at him. Far background, rue de Lancry: a third-floor window, an old man at an easel, tiny, never lit brighter than the street.*
> ✎ MISTRAL
> *Paris, 11 novembre, il tombe des cordes. Une valise, un courrier de notaire et une clé qui n'est pas encore la tienne.*

**a.p2** — *Inside Le Mistral. The door's bell. Heads turn. Gus is still standing on the bench, one hand raised, and freezes mid-story. Marin holds a spoon of soup halfway to his mouth. Lila, chin on her fist, is already interested.*
**Augustin « Gus » de Roncourt** — «…Et le duc, se penchant vers moi, de me souffler : "Augustin, vous êtes d'une élégance qui n'est plus de ce siècle."»

**Lila Bonnet** — «Penses-tu.»

**a.p3** *(silence)* — *The zinc, close. Toi's hand sets down the envelope, and on top of it the key ring: an old key and a small enamel cicada, green and gold. Margaux's hand, drying a glass, stops. We see only her hand and the cicada.*

**a.p4** — *Gus steps down from the bench and crosses the room with a bow that is too deep. The group watches behind him.*
**Augustin « Gus » de Roncourt** — «Tiens, tiens ! L'émissaire de M. Marchand, si je ne m'abuse ? On vous attendait… comme on attend la grêle.»

**Lila Bonnet** — «Regarde-moi ces pompes, Marin. Jamais un type de chez Solvel ne mettrait ça.»

**a.p6** — *Gus leans on the zinc beside Toi in his three-piece suit, all charm and no warmth. Toi's balloon, empty, is drawn from behind Toi's shoulder.*

**a.hook · midpoint** — *The bell again. In the doorway stands a figure in a rain-soaked bike helmet, holding up a key: Camille, seen from the side, not yet in close-up. On the zinc in the foreground lies Toi's key with its cicada. Two keys in one frame. Gus looks from one to the other.*
