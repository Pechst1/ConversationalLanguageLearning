## Jour 1 — T1 · Le mauvais accueil · jour A · mercredi 11 novembre

**a.p1** — *Night rain on the quai de Valmy. The red neon MISTRAL on the corner. Toi from behind, with a suitcase and an envelope held under the coat against the rain. **a.p2** — *Inside Le Mistral. The door's bell. Heads turn. Gus is still standing on the bench, one hand raised, and freezes mid-story. Marin holds a spoon of soup halfway to his mouth. Lila, chin on her fist, is already interested.*
