# Valid spellings that are both right (#250, package V stage 1).
#
# The 1990 rectifications («Les rectifications de l'orthographe», Journal officiel,
# 6 Dec 1990) made a second spelling of these words correct. School books in France,
# Belgium, Switzerland and Quebec teach the new one; dictionaries list both. A learner
# who writes either one is right, and no rule and no model may turn one into the other.
#
# Format: form_a | form_b | a sentence with {} where the word goes.
# The test puts each form into the sentence and asks for zero edits, then asks the
# model-edit check to refuse turning one form into the other.

# The circumflex on i and u is no longer required (except dû, mûr, sûr, jeûne and verb endings).
