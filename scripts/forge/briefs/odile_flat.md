# Chez Odile: the flat on the first floor above Le Mistral

The fourth room the forge writes from the canon, and the one the season turns on: Odile's flat,
two rooms on the first floor of Le Mistral's building, over the café's corner. It is built in its
T2 state (Day A, «Là-haut»): the shutters open, the morning light off the canal, the calendar
still on April 2023, the shoebox of photos pulled out from under the bed. The kitchen is the one
the stairwell shows through the door «1» (same walls, same things, same places), and the
bedroom lies south of it along the quai, under the two windows of the façade's first two bays
(floor at 4.6 m, the stairwell's landing). The place stays `locked` in `world.json`; the story
opens it.

## What the canon gives

- **The places file** (`docs/story/season-1/10-lieux-et-images.md`): «A small two-room Paris flat
  on the first floor above a corner café, stopped in April 2023: the shutters closed, dust
  suspended in thin bars of light, a woman's green coat on a hook by the door, felt slippers side
  by side under a chair, an old Polaroid camera on the table beside a cup with a dried ring of
  café crème, and a kitchen wall calendar showing April 2023. In the kitchen, above the gas
  stove, one patch of wall is painted a whiter white than the rest. A narrow balcony looks over
  the canal, and a shoebox sits under the made bed.» Its states: «T2: shutters open: morning
  light, dust, the calendar, the Polaroid box spilling on the floorboards.» Its palette: paper
  and dust-cobalt; with the shutters open, sunflower light off the canal. Never mess, decay or
  horror: «a room that waited, not a room that rotted».
- **T2 Day A** (`02-la-haut.md`): P2 the doorway (the coat, the camera beside the cup, the
  slippers under the chair); P3 Lila opens the shutters and the light floods the room; P4 the
  calendar's entries in Odile's hand («12/4 dentiste», «Tous les dimanches : M.», «3/5
  anniversaire Marin — tarte aux pommes», «20/5 coiffeur»); P8 and P9 the shoebox from under the
  bed, the photos on the floorboards, their captions; Day B P7 the narrow balcony at night.

The forge's own choices, not canon: the plan (the kitchen as the stairwell has it, 4.5 m by
3.9 m; the bedroom beside it on the quai, 4.5 m by 5.4 m, and the doorway between them), the
tomettes and the boards, the walls' cream and dust-cobalt, the double bed under its cream
counterpane, the wardrobe, which window has the balcony (the one over the gap in the plane trees
by the café's corner, so it sees the water), and what the canal shows (the quai's own plan: its
trees, its lamps, the zebra to the corner, the quai de Jemmapes and houses with no names on
their shops). It is morning: the T2 scene is at half past eight. The leaves are November's.

The stairwell's kitchen had its doorway to the next room in its west wall, where the building
has no window to give a bedroom. It is now in the south wall, toward the quai, in both files.

## How it plays

You come in by the door from the landing, shut behind you, with the window in front and the light
on the tiles: «Chez Odile, au premier. Les volets sont ouverts : la lumière vient du canal.» Tap
the coat, the calendar (its April entries), the whiter patch above the cooker, the camera, the cup,
and through the doorway the bed and the photos on the floor (one caption read out). «Rentrer» and
the end card says what you looked at. Nobody is there: Lila and her croissants are the stage's.
The calendar's May entries are for T2's solve («Elle avait tout prévu ? Montre.»), so the tap
reads only April's; Lila's own photo and Augustin's are not on the floor (she pockets them).

## Still plain

- **Nobody is there**: no Lila, no croissants, no second cup set beside Odile's (P6). The stage
  places people.
- **No dust in the light**: the beam is told by the sun on the floor, not by motes.
- **The balcony is seen, not stood on**: the French window does not open, and the quai's own
  façade has no balcony on the first floor yet (the quai's look is kept as it is).
- **The canal has no mirror** in a room: the water is flat green, no barge, no lock.
- **The corner window** on the chamfer and the two on the rue de Lancry, shut on the quai, are in
  walls this plan does not reach.
- **Later states** (Lila's easel, the crate, the night lamp and the notebook, the two calendars)
  are not built: this is T2's morning.
- **Photos.** None: no open photograph shows this flat, which the canon invents; the canon is the
  reference.
