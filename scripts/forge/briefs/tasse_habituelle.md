# Ta tasse habituelle: the learner's usual on Le Mistral's zinc

The first of Le Mistral's details, after the season's rooms: the learner's usual order, served on
the zinc in front of where you stand, once the story knows it. Its file is
`web-frontend/lib/quartier/world/details/tasse_habituelle.json`; it waits on the season flag
`user.usual_order` and draws the cup that order is served in.

## What the canon gives

- **Gap 1 · 3 «La même chose ?»** (`docs/story/season-1/09-entre-les-episodes.md`): «Margaux
  asks your order for the third time. Gus insists your order says who you are. You choose, and
  say why you're still here. *The change:* your usual order exists. Gus announces that you are
  «un habitué», and it is not a joke.» The day plan settles the fact when the learner did not
  choose: «the usual order is a café crème».
- **The season bible**: `user.usual_order`, text, set in Gap 1, read in T5 A and the gaps:
  «Small moments: who remembers it».
- **T5 A · Day A P4** (`05-berlin.md`): «Lila behind the zinc […] She puts a cup in front of
  you: your usual, exactly right (from `user.usual_order`).»
- The values the season's bridges offer (`app/data/season/s1/bridges.json`): «un café crème»,
  «un noisette», «un thé», «un chocolat chaud».

What T1 already stages (the key on the zinc, Odile's café crème at her place) is not a detail,
and Gap 1's studio soup is in the studio, not in Le Mistral: so the usual cup is the first.

The forge's own choices, not canon: the cup by what it holds (a wide cup for a café crème, a
small one for a noisette or an espresso, a tea cup with the bag's string and tag over the rim, a
big cup for a chocolat), the saucer, the spoon and a wrapped sugar beside it, the handle to your
right, and its place on the zinc, a quarter of a metre in from the front edge where you stand
(`MARKS.atZinc`). An order the forge does not know is served as a café crème's wide cup.

## Round 1 (r1, 844×844, 4 orders × at the zinc, close, the room)

- The saucer, the spoon and the sugar were sunk in the zinc: the cup stood at 1.242 m, the
  zinc's top is at 1.26 m. Only the cup showed.
- The cup's inside was a porcelain floor just under the rim that hid the drink: every order read
  as the same pale cup. The tea read as milk.

## Round 2 (r2, and r2-phone at 390×844)

The cup sits on its saucer on the zinc, the spoon in front, the sugar beside. The inside goes
down into the cup and the drink is its own colour: a café crème's tan, a noisette's darker
brown, the tea's amber with the string over the rim, the chocolat's dark brown in a bigger cup.
From where you stand it reads at a glance, on a phone too.

## Played

The standalone page with `?detail=tasse_habituelle&value=<each order>`: the cup is on the zinc
in front of you, the room as it was. No errors.
