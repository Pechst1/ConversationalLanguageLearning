# Ton studio: the short-let on the sixth floor

The second room the forge writes from the canon, and the first that is yours: the studio you rent
for the week, under the roofs, six floors up with no lift. It is one room (3.3 m by 3.8 m, the
door from the landing on the south, the window onto the courtyard on the north), on the
sixth floor of a building off the quai (floor at 19.75 m, the stairwell's storey heights). The
place stays `locked` in `world.json`, inside the quai, with no pin of its own: the story opens it.

## What the canon gives

- **The places file** (`docs/story/season-1/10-lieux-et-images.md`): «A sixth-floor walk-up studio
  with the cold radiator (canon) and a phone lit on the bed. The return ticket is on its screen.»
- **T1, last panel** (`01-le-mauvais-accueil.md`): «Your rented studio. The radiator is cold
  (canon), and you sit on the bed in your coat. On the phone screen: «Retour : mercredi 18
  novembre». The key ring lies next to the phone, the cicada face up.»
- **The bible**: you arrive with a suitcase, the notary's envelope and a key on Odile's key ring
  with its small enamel cicada; the return ticket is booked a week later; the short-let studio
  has the cold radiator.
- **The week after** (`12-la-semaine-dapres.md`): Marin at the door with a pot of soup (ep1.a.l.p2);
  a postcard on the doormat on Sunday (ep1.b.p4). The door and its mat are where those land.

The forge's own choices, not canon: the plan and its size, the boards, the kitchenette (a sink,
two hot plates, a shelf), the table with two chairs under the window, the bare bulb in a paper
globe, the suitcase by the door, the courtyard and the roofs across it. The phone reads only what
the canon writes on it.

## How it plays

You arrive with the light on (no minuterie here: a room without a switch is lit from the start)
and the caption says where you are and that it is cold. Tap the phone, the radiator, the key ring
or the suitcase and each says what it is in a line; nothing moves, nothing opens. «Rentrer» ends
the visit, and the end card says what you looked at («Tu as touché le radiateur : il est
froid.»), or that you looked round the room. Nothing in the lines agrees an adjective with you.

## Round 1 (r1, 844×844, load 0.23 s, 9k triangles, 6 views)

- **What reads.** The bed with its grey blanket and the phone and keys on it, the radiator under
  the window, the door with its panels, the plan.
- **The arrival** looked down at the bed corner from the door: nothing of the window, the table
  or the radiator. The start now looks across the room at the window: table and chair on the
  left, the radiator under the glass, the bed and its phone on the right.
- **The window** showed only the façade across the court, its mansard above the frame: a court
  of six storeys stands as tall as your own. Five storeys put its zinc, its dormers and its
  chimney pots at your eye, which is the sixth floor's view.
- **The kitchenette** seemed to stand outside the west wall in the plan. It does not: the plan is
  a perspective from above, and a worktop at 0.9 m and a shelf at 1.55 m are nearer its lens than
  the floor. The local frame was checked (front to the room, the sink on your left as you face it).
- **The phone** was a speck seen from standing height. Its view now closes the lens on it the way
  a hand brings it up (`forge-view.ts`, phones only), and the screen reads «Retour :» and
  «mercredi 18 novembre».

## Round 2 (r2, 6 views, load 0.13 s)

## Round 3 (r3, 390×844, the phone's frame)

Played upright, the arrival had the window full and the bed cut off at the right edge. The start
looks a little further right: the window, the radiator under it and the head of the bed, so the
cold reads first and the bed is a turn of the thumb away. The hint at the bottom of the screen
spoke of the orange button, which this room does not have: a room without a switch now says
«touche une chose pour la regarder», and its title card says the same.

The cover, the arrival with the light on and its caption («Ton studio, au sixième sans
ascenseur. Il fait froid.»), then a tap on each thing where it shows on the screen, turning to the
ones behind you: the radiator («Le radiateur est froid.»), the phone («Sur l'écran : «Retour :
mercredi 18 novembre». Ton billet de retour.»), the key ring («La clé d'Odile, et la cigale sur

card lists the four things you did. No errors; about 9k triangles.

## Still plain

- **You are not on the bed in your coat.** The canon's panel has you sitting; the stage has no
  body of yours to sit, so the room is seen from the door, standing.
- **The cold is told, not shown**: no breath in the air, no frost on the glass.
- **The notary's envelope** is not on the table: the canon does not place it in the studio.
- **The boards** are the kit's warm oak, brighter than a top-floor room's would be.
- **The court** is the kit's: plain façades and a mansard, no skylight open, no canal.
- **Photos.** None: no open photograph shows a short-let's inside, and the canon is the reference.
