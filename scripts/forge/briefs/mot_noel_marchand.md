# Le mot de Noël pour Marchand: against the desk lamp, the paper bag beside it

On 28 Nov `forge.py queue --season --from api` put the learners on g4 and g6 and printed `next:
none`. `queue --orders` printed a new order, so the run lays it: `next detail: «le mot de Noël
pour Marchand» in gerance_marchand (mark: marchand_office)`, one life, its look «A small card
propped against the lamp on the desk, with the paper bag beside it.»

## What the canon gives

- **«Deux croissants»** (gap 6, `09-entre-les-episodes.md`): «Christmas morning: a paper bag at
  Odile's door with two croissants in it. You go up to thank Marchand and find the office empty,
  the camp bed stripped. Camille took him home. You leave something in return, and choose what.»
  The change: the Sunday ritual has passed to the learner.
- **The office** (`world/rooms/gerance_marchand.json`): the desk with its green blotter and the
  files at one end, the green desk lamp on it, its foot behind on the sitter's side, the framed
  Polaroid «Dimanche. M. rit. Enfin.».
- **Vous.** Marchand is a stranger to the learner: vous, or no pronoun at all.

## The forge's own choices, not canon

- **The card.** A small cream card, 9 by 14 cm, a sprig of holly at its top, the words in a quick
  blue ballpoint hand: «Joyeux Noël, M. Marchand. Merci pour les croissants !». No signature:
  the learner's name is not written in the world.
- **Where.** It leans against the lamp's stem from the blotter's side, its foot just off the
  lamp's round foot, turned toward the visitor's chair. Facing the visitor straight on, the card
  sat right under the shade and could not be read from anywhere a learner stands.
- **The bag.** A baker's brown paper bag, its top folded over twice, on the blotter beside the
  card: the bag the croissants came in, the learner's something in return inside it. What is
  inside is not drawn; the story chose it.
- The camp bed stays made, as the room has it: the room is not changed by a detail.

## The kit

- `notecard` and `paperbag`, new in `room.ts` and `scripts/forge/room.py`'s list; the card's art
  is `noteCardArt()`.

## Rounds

1. The card faced the visitor's side, leaned on the stem: from the desk it was a white speck
   under the shade, and from close by the shade hid it. The bag read as a cardboard box.
2. The card moved round to the blotter's side of the lamp's foot, turned to the visitor's chair,
   leaned further back (0.6 rad) to rest on the stem; the bag slimmer and paler. The holly read,
   the words were too small.
3. Shorter lines («M. Marchand.») in a larger, firmer hand: «Joyeux Noël, M. Marchand. Merci pour
   les croissants !» reads close up and on a phone.
