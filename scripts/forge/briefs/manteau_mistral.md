`forge.py queue --season --from api` on 24 Nov printed `next: none`, so the run takes the next

## What the canon gives

## The forge's own choices, not canon

- **«Odile's portrait».** No portrait of Odile hung in that corner, and the order speaks of one
  as there, so it is laid with the coat: a Polaroid, as the canon wants her. It is not the photo
  by the booth (`dessin_maitresse`) a second time: it is her by day, before the window, turned the
  other way, captioned «Odile, au Mistral» on its foot in blue ballpoint.
- **The coat.** The kit's `coat` on its brass hook, in navy (`tone: navy`), so it is never
  mistaken for Odile's green coat in the stairwell. Nothing in its pockets: the learner's return
  ticket is on the phone in the canon (an airline's, with SMS reminders), so no paper ticket.
- **Where.** «The corner above the zinc»: the north wall between the zinc's north end and the
  booth's end, under the clock over the zinc's far end. The hook is at 1.86 m, the hem clear of
  the floor and of the booth's end panel; the Polaroid just above the hook.
